In this guide, you'll learn how to use [Puter](https://puter.com/) to deploy your static Nuxt app for free. Puter is a cloud operating system, with hosting, cloud storage, and much more built in, allowing you to easily deploy your Nuxt app.

## Deploying your Nuxt App

### Step 1: Prepare Your App

Inside your Nuxt project folder, simply run the command to generate your website files:

```javascript
npx nuxi generate
```

After the build completes, your website files will be in the `.output/public` folder. This is the folder you'll upload to Puter.

![Nuxt static output folder](/assets/img/nuxt/output.webp)

### Step 2: Upload Your App to Puter

1. Go to [Puter.com](https://puter.com)
2. Drag the `public` folder from your local machine into Puter cloud desktop

![Uploaded files](/assets/img/nuxt/drag.webp)

1. Open the folder to verify that your Nuxt app is uploaded

![Uploaded files](/assets/img/nuxt/uploaded.webp)

### Step 3: Publish Your Nuxt App

1. In the same folder, right-click and select  **Publish as Website**

![Context Menu: Publish as Website](/assets/img/nuxt/publish-website.webp)

1. Choose your free subdomain (e.g., `yoursite.puter.site`) and click  **Publish**

![Choose free subdomain](/assets/img/nuxt/subdomain.webp)

1. Congratulations, you have successfully published your Nuxt app!

![Publish website](/assets/img/nuxt/published.webp)

## Free Nuxt Hosting with Puter

[Host your Nuxt app for free on Puter](/static-hosting/) by simply uploading your files and publishing them as a website, no complicated configuration or setup required. Once published, your site is instantly accessible on the internet, and updating it is just as easy: simply replace your files.

Beyond hosting, Puter.js lets you add databases, [AI](/ai/), [cloud storage](/object-storage/), and more to your website without requiring any API keys.

Find out more about Puter.js in the [documentation](https://docs.puter.com).
