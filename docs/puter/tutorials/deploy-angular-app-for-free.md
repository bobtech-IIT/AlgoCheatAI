In this guide, you'll learn how to use [Puter](https://puter.com/) to deploy your Angular app for free. Puter is a cloud operating system in your browser with a web hosting feature allowing you to host your website easily.

## Deploying your Angular App

### Step 1: Prepare Your Site

Inside your Angular project folder, simply run the build command to generate your website files:

```javascript
npm run build
```

After the build completes, your website files will be in the `dist/<project_name>/browser` folder. This `browser` folder is what you'll upload to Puter.

![Angular static output folder](/assets/img/angular/output.webp)

### Step 2: Upload Your Site to Puter

1. Go to [Puter.com](https://puter.com)
2. Drag the `browser` folder from your local machine into the Puter cloud desktop

![Uploaded files](/assets/img/angular/drag.webp)

1. Open the folder to verify that your Angular app is uploaded

![Uploaded files](/assets/img/angular/uploaded.webp)

### Step 3: Publish Your Angular App

1. In the same folder, right-click and select  **Publish as Website**

![Context Menu: Publish as Website](/assets/img/angular/publish-website.webp)

1. Choose your free subdomain (e.g., `yoursite.puter.site`) and click  **Publish**

![Choose free subdomain](/assets/img/angular/subdomain.webp)

1. Congratulations, you have successfully published your Angular app!

![Publish website](/assets/img/angular/published.webp)

## Free Angular Hosting with Puter

[With Puter, you can easily host your Angular app](/static-hosting/). Simply upload your files and publish them as a website.

Your website is instantly accessible on the internet, without any complex configuration or servers. Updating your website is just a matter of replacing your files. No dashboards to navigate or technical setup required.

In addition to hosting, you can integrate databases, [AI capabilities](/ai/), [cloud storage](/object-storage/), and more into your website with Puter.js, without managing backend infrastructure.

Find out more about Puter.js in the [documentation](https://docs.puter.com).
